# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone. Participation in this project should be harassment-free regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

We pledge to foster a community built on professionalism, collaboration, and mutual respect.

---

# Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Being respectful and considerate toward others.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them learn.
- Providing constructive feedback professionally.
- Accepting feedback with maturity and openness.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on improving the project for everyone.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks, insults, or abusive language.
- Trolling or deliberately provoking conflict.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or unwanted advances.
- Disruptive or unprofessional conduct.
- Any behavior that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

---

# Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct fairly and consistently.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, or other contributions that violate these guidelines. Enforcement decisions will be communicated when appropriate.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Wiki pages
- Project documentation
- Community chats
- Social media interactions representing the project
- Conferences or events related to the project

---

# Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer.

If a dedicated contact email is available, use it. Otherwise, contact the maintainer through GitHub.

Please include:

- Description of the incident
- Date and time
- Relevant screenshots or links (if applicable)
- Any additional context

Reports will be handled confidentially whenever possible.

---

# Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may apply one or more of the following actions depending on the severity and frequency of violations.

## 1. Correction

**Community Impact**

Minor inappropriate behavior or language.

**Consequence**

A private warning explaining the issue and expected future behavior.

---

## 2. Warning

**Community Impact**

Repeated inappropriate behavior or a more serious single incident.

**Consequence**

A formal warning. Continued violations may result in temporary suspension.

---

## 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**

Serious misconduct or repeated violations.

**Consequence**

Temporary suspension from participating in the project community.

---

## 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**

Harassment, discrimination, threats, or repeated severe misconduct.

**Consequence**

Permanent removal from all project spaces and future participation.
